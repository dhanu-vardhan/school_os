1. Open home as director; check dashboard tiles.
2. Switch to teacher; open roster; mark attendance.
3. Switch to guardian; open payments; open activities.
4. Switch to reception; open kiosk; verify authorised/restricted states.
5. Switch to safeguarding; open concerns; verify restricted styling.
